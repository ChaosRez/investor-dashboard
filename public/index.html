New Investor Onboarding Guide: Where to Start & Portfolio Stages

Building an ETF portfolio does not require predicting markets. Follow this stage-by-stage retail checklist to understand what is mandatory, what is optional, and how to maintain your portfolio with zero stress.

🏁 Stage 1: Core Foundation (START HERE — Mandatory)
Goal: Establish your core wealth building engine
Essential for Beginners

Start in the ETF Screener. Most retail investors need only ONE single global fund ("VWCE and Chill" or Invesco FWRA), or a two-fund split (88% IWDA + 12% EIMI). Ensure:

  • Accumulating (Acc): Never buy Distributing unless you rely on live dividend income. Acc avoids annual tax leakage!
  • Fund Size (AUM) > €1B: Protects against fund closure and ensures institutional bid-ask spreads.
  • Ireland Domicile (IE): Shields you from 40% US Estate Tax and cuts US dividend withholding from 30% to 15%.
🔍 Stage 2: Overlap & Redundancy Audit (Mandatory if holding >1 fund)
Goal: Avoid unintended tech mega-cap concentration
Essential for Multi-Fund Portfolios

Beginners often buy an S&P 500 ETF, an MSCI World ETF, and a Tech ETF, mistakenly believing they have diversified. In reality, they are holding 30%+ in just Apple, Microsoft, and NVIDIA across three wrappers. Use the Overlap Auditor to detect single-stock clustering before committing capital.

📈 Stage 3: Wealth Accumulation & DCA Plan (Planning Phase)
Goal: Structure monthly automated contributions
Planning Phase

Set up a monthly automated savings plan (Sparplan) at your broker (e.g. Trade Republic, Scalable, ING). Use the Curvo DCA Engine to simulate your 10–30 year timeline and see real inflation-adjusted purchasing power.

🧮 Stage 4: Risk Tuning & Factor Tilts (OPTIONAL — Advanced Investors)
Goal: Dampen volatility or add academic factor tilts
Optional Step

This stage is completely optional. If your stomach churns during market downturns, add 10%–20% Global Aggregate Bonds (AGGU EUR Hedged). Its near-zero correlation (0.12) cushions drawdowns along the Markowitz Efficient Frontier. If you seek factor premiums, consider a modest 10% tilt toward Small-Cap Value (IUSN / ZPRV).

📡 Stage 5: News Radar & Maintenance (Ongoing Phase)
Goal: Stay informed without reacting emotionally
Maintenance Phase

Use the Market News Radar to review macro and central bank rate signals. Remember the #1 Bogleheads rule: Never sell out of panic or pause your DCA. Rebalance only once a year if your equity/bond split drifts by more than 5%.

📑 Stage 6: Statutory Check Before Buying (Mandatory Compliance)
Goal: Verify official legal documentation
Mandatory Before Purchasing

Before transferring money to your broker, open the Official Documents & KIDs Hub and download the statutory Key Information Document (KID) to confirm synthetic risk level (SRI 1–7) and ongoing transaction charges.
